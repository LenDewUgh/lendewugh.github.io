Questions

Before you buy.

Why manual entry?

Typing the number is how you notice it. It also means the app never asks for a bank credential, because there is nothing to connect.

Does it sync?

Across your own devices through your iCloud. Nothing reaches a LenDew server, because there isn't one.

Is there a subscription?

No. $9.99 once, every tab, forever.

Can I get my data out?

Yes — PDF export from any month, any time. It's your ledger.

We can't read it.
That's the design.

Everything you enter is stored on your device and, if you have iCloud enabled, in your own private iCloud database. LenDew has no server and cannot see it. Read the full Sus Spend privacy policy.
