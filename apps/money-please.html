Privacy Policy

Effective: March 2025

Overview

Money Please ("the App") is built with privacy at its core. The App does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information. All data you enter remains solely on your device.

Data Collection

Money Please does not collect any personal data. No names, device identifiers, usage analytics, crash reports, or financial information is gathered, stored externally, or transmitted to any server or third party.

Local Storage Only

All information you enter — expenses, categories, amounts — is stored exclusively on your local device and is never sent anywhere. This data exists solely to power the App's functionality.

Third-Party Services

Money Please does not integrate with any third-party analytics platforms, advertising networks, or external services of any kind.

Children's Privacy

Money Please does not collect data from any users. It is intended for general audiences and complies with applicable children's privacy laws.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the new version will be posted at this URL. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please reach out via the App Store support link on the Money Please listing page.
