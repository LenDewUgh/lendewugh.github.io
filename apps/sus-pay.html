We can't read it.
That's the design.

Your trips, rates and pay settings are stored on your device and, if iCloud is enabled, in your own private iCloud database. Roster PDFs and calendar events are parsed on the device; nothing is uploaded to LenDew, because LenDew has no server. Read the full Sus Pay privacy policy.
