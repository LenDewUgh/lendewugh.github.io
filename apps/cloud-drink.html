Privacy Policy

Effective: March 2025

Overview

Cloud Drink ("the App") is designed with privacy at its core. The App does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information about you or your passengers. All data entered into the App remains solely on your device.

Data Collection

Cloud Drink does not collect any personal data. No names, device identifiers, usage analytics, crash reports, or any other information is gathered, stored externally, or transmitted to any server or third party.

Local Storage Only

Any information you enter into Cloud Drink — such as seat numbers or drink orders — is stored exclusively on your local device and is never sent anywhere. This data is used solely to power the App's functionality during your session.

Third-Party Services

Cloud Drink does not integrate with any third-party analytics platforms, advertising networks, or external services. No SDKs that collect data are included in the App.

Children's Privacy

Cloud Drink is a professional tool intended for use by adult flight attendants. It does not collect any data from any users, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the new version will be posted at this URL. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or the App, please reach out via the App Store support link on the Cloud Drink listing page.
