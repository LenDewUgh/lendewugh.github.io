Support

One person answers this.

Every LenDew app is made and supported by one person. Email lendewugh@icloud.com with the app's name and what happened, and you'll hear back — usually within a couple of days, sooner if it's broken.

Before you write

Setting up Sus Pay? The step-by-step Sus Pay guide walks through connecting your work calendar, importing trips and reading your pay.

Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple, not by us: open the App Store, tap your picture, then Purchase History. Refund requests go through reportaproblem.apple.com.

Sync in Sus Spend and Sus Pay runs through your own iCloud. If devices disagree, check that both are signed into the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive on.

Invites from Sus Spend and Sus Pay are iCloud share links. They open in the app they came from; if the app isn't installed yet, the link shows an App Store button first.

LOSA isn't on the public App Store. Trained observers can email the address above to be issued a TestFlight invite.
